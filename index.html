Catch the fall
on a $9 sensor.

Falls are the leading cause of injury death over 65. This project runs a CNN+RNN fall detector on ESP32-cam devices — no raw video ever leaves the room. A temporal GRU reads the dynamics of a fall, slashing the false positives that plague single-frame systems.

By Ho Ko · Yueqi Liu · Jiaxin Xie — ECE, University of Waterloo

Stage 1 — CNN

A MobileNet-style depthwise-separable CNN extracts a 64-d spatial feature per frame at ~8–9× fewer FLOPs than standard convolutions.

128×128 → DepthwiseSep×3 → GAP → f∈ℝ⁶⁴
⏱️

Stage 2 — GRU

A 30-frame window (~1s) feeds a GRU that captures the vertical→horizontal→still signature — distinguishing a fall from intentional lying down.

[f₋₂₉ … f₀] → GRU(64) → σ → P(fall)
