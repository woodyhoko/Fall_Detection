Fall Detection — interactive optical-flow + RNN demo

Drag the person around the scene and use the body-tilt slider — or hit Trigger a fall. The demo measures real optical flow (frame-to-frame pixel motion), plus the figure's downward velocity, acceleration spike and how horizontal it is, then feeds that sequence into a small RNN-style temporal model. When the motion actually looks like a fall — fast drop, impact deceleration, body going horizontal — the fall probability climbs and the alert fires.

⚠️ Simplified example. The figure is a drawn silhouette and the recurrent model uses small hand-set weights (it isn't the trained CNN+RNN from the paper). The optical-flow and acceleration signals it reads are computed for real from your interaction — the point is to show why temporal dynamics make a fall detectable, not to reproduce the deployed ESP32-cam pipeline.
